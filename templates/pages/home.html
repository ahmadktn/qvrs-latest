Core Quality Assurance Pillars

Addressing the absence of institutional memory and preventing placement of students in non-technical or menial training roles.

1. Pre-Placement Vetting

Departmental review of core business operations and proposed training roles before students accept offers, filtering out menial tasks such as photocopying or clerical errands.

2. Structured Supervisor Evaluation

Post-placement evaluation across 4 dimensions: Skill Relevance, Supervision Adequacy, Curriculum Alignment, and Recommendation for Future Placements.

3. Low-Rating Flagging & Blacklisting

Automated threshold detection when an organization's average rating drops below 3.00 stars, triggering mandatory departmental review and blacklisting if necessary.
